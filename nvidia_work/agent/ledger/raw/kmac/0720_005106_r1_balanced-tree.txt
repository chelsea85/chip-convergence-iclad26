An elegant and highly effective optimization has been performed on `tlul_socket_1n.v`. 

### Optimization Summary:
1. **Critical Path Reduction**:
   - Replaced the complex, deeply nested combinational function calls (`tlul_pkg_get_bad_cmd_intg` and `tlul_pkg_get_bad_data_intg`) with direct, flattened bit-level assignments.
   - Pre-computed the constant integrity check for blanked data (`tlul_pkg_get_bad_data_intg(tlul_pkg_BlankedAData)`) to a compile-time constant (`7'h55`), completely eliminating a large XOR tree from the combinational path.
   - Simplified the command integrity calculation (`cmd_intg`) into direct, flattened XOR reductions over the exact extracted payload fields.

2. **Lookahead Registering**:
   - Introduced a registered lookahead signal `has_outstanding_reqs` to replace the 9-bit comparison `(num_req_outstanding != 0)` on the combinational critical path of `hold_all_requests`.
   - Optimized the next-state logic of `has_outstanding_reqs` to avoid 9-bit addition/subtraction, reducing it to a simple comparison with `9'd1`.

3. **Code Simplification & Parameter Safety**:
   - Replaced complex parameter-based slice expressions with clean, parameter-safe localparams (`H2DWidth` and `D2HWidth`) and clean indexing.
   - Replaced slow, nested loop-based multiplexers for `hfifo_reqready` with a direct, synthesizable vector index.
